About Physics Archives

Physics Archives is an independent educational initiative dedicated to preserving, documenting, and sharing the history of physics. Our mission is to create one of the world's most comprehensive digital archives of physicists, discoveries, historical documents, Nobel Prizes, experiments, and milestones that have shaped modern science.

Milestones in Physics

Explore some of the landmark discoveries that transformed our understanding of nature.

1687

Newton's Principia

Isaac Newton publishes Philosophiæ Naturalis Principia Mathematica, laying the foundation of classical mechanics.

1900

Birth of Quantum Theory

Max Planck introduces the quantum hypothesis while studying black-body radiation.

1905

Einstein's Miracle Year

Albert Einstein publishes groundbreaking papers on relativity, Brownian motion, and the photoelectric effect.

1925–1927

Quantum Mechanics

Heisenberg, Schrödinger, Born, Dirac and others establish modern quantum mechanics.

2012

Higgs Boson

CERN announces the discovery of the Higgs boson, confirming the final missing particle of the Standard Model.
